# Conditional and independent-pair GP loss-weight search

The trained objective is `L_conditional + lambda * L_pair`. The original term is the per-query conditional joint Gaussian negative log likelihood of random context episodes. These episodes are not generated by the acquisition policy. The second term is the mean marginal Gaussian negative log likelihood of 64 independently sampled within-series pairs. Series sampling mass is proportional to compound count. Both the neural pair mean and conventional covariance kernel receive gradients. No sign-consistency or cycle-consistency penalty is used.

The expanded coarse grid contains 0, 0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10, 30, and 100. Upper-bound winners receive 300 and 1000. Geometric midpoints refine around the winning seed11 weights. Each endpoint/fold promotes its two best seed11 weights, the previous selected weight, and zero to three-seed comparison. Mean validation likelihood across seeds11,29,47 selects the final coefficient. The existing checkpoint schedule and validation weighting remain unchanged.

All configurations use the previous selected neural-mean/conventional-kernel architecture, training-only PCA, outcome scaling, 250 mean warm-up steps, and 2400 joint training updates. Reusable original and auxiliary checkpoints are referenced without modifying them. Remaining runs train from the same initialization and random episode stream for each seed. This is a loss-weight search with matched training budgets, not additional fine-tuning epochs on the previous checkpoint.

Outer test data are evaluated only after the search is locked. The three predictor comparisons are zero auxiliary weight, the previous search winner among 0.1/0.3/1.0, and the expanded search winner. Prediction and expected-improvement (EI) acquisition are evaluated at three GP seeds. Matched PPO is retrained at three policy seeds with GP seed11 fixed and previous auxiliary-PPO optimizer hyperparameters. Earlier PPO results supply the fixed baselines. The original and prior auxiliary search were selected with seed11; expanded selection also uses validation seed stability. This additional selection robustness is part of the intervention.

Reported predictor diagnostics include all-pair differences, fixed-hidden-query context learning, and raw versus validation-calibrated uncertainty. Acquisition metrics are only purchases to any top1/2/3/4 compound. The primary population has at least15 compounds per pool. Smaller pools and size-cutoff curves are included. Paired linked-group bootstrap intervals average seeds and starts before resampling; they are conditional on fitted models.

Loss coefficients are not data percentages or gradient percentages. Both losses are averaged per target, but joint conditional and marginal gradients differ in scale and direction. Diagnostics measure their norms and alignment separately for mean and kernel parameters. Keeping the original additive loss also preserves its gradient clipping behavior, so extremely large coefficients affect optimization scale as well as task balance. A selected upper-bound coefficient indicates an off-policy-dominated objective, not proof of a finite optimum.
